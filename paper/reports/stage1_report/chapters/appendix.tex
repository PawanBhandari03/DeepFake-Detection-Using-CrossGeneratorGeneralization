\chapter{Glossary}
\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering\small
\caption{Terms and abbreviations used in this report.}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{3.6cm}X@{}}
\toprule
Term & Meaning\\
\midrule
Generator & An AI tool that creates images or videos, for example Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, the ChatGPT image model or the Gemini image model\\
Cross-generator generalization & Detecting content from generators that were not used in training\\
CLIP & A pretrained vision-language model from OpenAI; its frozen image encoder provides the features\\
Frozen encoder & A model whose weights are not changed during our training\\
Trust Score & $100\times(1-p)$, where $p$ is the calibrated probability of AI generation; 100 means that the file looks authentic\\
ROC-AUC & Area under the ROC curve; 0.5 means guessing and 1.0 means a perfect ranking of fake above real\\
Calibration & Making a probability match the real frequency of the event\\
Platt scaling & Fitting $p=\sigma(a s+c)$ on validation data to calibrate a raw score\\
Patch & A $224\times224$ pixel square cut from the image\\
Heatmap & A map that colours each patch by how AI-like it looks\\
ELA, SRM, DCT & Error-level analysis, spatial rich model noise residuals, discrete cosine transform features\\
FR / NFR & Functional / non-functional requirement\\
\bottomrule
